A remote for the music you own, built into the player you already use.

Your phone in your hand.
Your music on the other player.

Put Naviamp on your computer or Android TV, then use another Naviamp device to browse your collection, choose a track, and control the queue.

The playback device streams directly from your music server. Your controller sends commands and follows what’s playing, so the music can keep going when you stop controlling it.

Naviamp Connect is a beta for Android and desktop. Android TV receives playback; phones and desktops can control or receive it. iOS Connect is not included in the current preview.

From two devices to one listening session.

  1. Open both players

    Use compatible Naviamp versions on reachable Android or desktop devices. Connect the playback device to the music source it will play.

  2. Pair in Controllers

    Open Settings → Controllers, find the other device, and follow the pairing-code and approval steps.

  3. Choose where to listen

    Select the paired player and start music. Its own Naviamp instance handles playback while you control the session.

What needs to be reachable?

The controller must be able to reach the playback device, and the playback device must be able to reach the music server. Automatic discovery works on the local network; a routed network may need manual pairing and suitable network permissions.

Queue handoff needs a compatible music source on the receiving device. Pairing can also offer an existing connection to help set up another player, with the required approval.

Connect has no hosted internet relay and needs no separate Naviamp account. Google Cast is a separate output option for compatible receivers. Connect remains beta while broader device and recovery testing continues.
