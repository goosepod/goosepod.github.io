Naviamp

A music player for people who run their own library. Connect to Navidrome or another OpenSubsonic-compatible server and use the same familiar player across computers and phones.

What it does

The useful parts, close at hand.

Browse your library

Albums, artists, tracks, genres, favorites, playlists, recent music, random picks, and internet radio.

Play without gaps

Gapless playback, ReplayGain, crossfade, waveform seeking, lyrics, queue controls, and detailed now-playing information.

Discover related music

Sonic Mix, Sonic Path, track radio, related tracks, and autoplay use server-side similarity when your server supports it.

Build playlists

Create regular and smart playlists, edit them in the app, and save generated queues back to your server.

Listen offline

Keep music and library information on your device for times when the server is unavailable.

Adjust the player

Choose compact layouts, colors, artwork treatments, waveforms, metadata, playback settings, and desktop visualizers.

About the project

Naviamp treats Navidrome as its first-class server target and also works with compatible OpenSubsonic servers. Your music stays on the server you choose.

  • No ads or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Credentials use operating-system secure storage where supported.
  • Unavailable server capabilities hide or fall back cleanly.
