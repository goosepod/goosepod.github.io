A lot more than play

The big features, close at hand.

Explore the whole library

Albums, artists, tracks, genres, favorites, recent music, random picks, rich search, and every credited artist.

Build a listening session

Artist, Album, and Genre Mixes plus Sonic Mix, Sonic Path, track radio, related music, and sonic autoplay on compatible servers.

Shape your playlists

Create and edit regular playlists, save generated queues, and build reusable smart playlists where the provider supports them.

Bring radio with you

Create, edit, browse, and play internet radio stations beside the rest of your collection, with live metadata when available.

Listen offline

Download music, manage storage and cache behavior, and switch to an offline dashboard away from the server.

Own the playback

Queues, waveform seeking, lyrics, ReplayGain, gapless playback, crossfade, sample-rate controls, favorites, ratings, and detailed metadata.

Make it look right

Album-art colors, Aurora gradients, blur, compact layouts, configurable metadata, waveforms, and reactive desktop visualizers.

Move between devices

A shared experience across desktop, Android, and iOS, with secure credentials, settings sync, and Android Auto support.

Open, private, and yours

Your library stays with the service or server you choose. Naviamp contains no ads or third-party tracking SDKs, and credentials use operating-system secure storage where supported.

Provider capabilities differ, so Naviamp hides or gracefully falls back from server-specific features instead of leaving broken controls behind.

Naviamp is available for macOS, Windows, Linux, and Android. An unsigned arm64 iPhone/iPad IPA preview is available for users who can provide their own signing.
