feedss is a small, focused RSS reader for keeping up with the sites you care about—without recommendations, tracking, or a hosted account.

Reading without ceremony

Everything important stays close at hand.

Organize subscriptions

Arrange feeds into groups, see unread totals at a glance, and move between groups or individual sources.

Choose how much to read

Use headlines, headline-and-summary, or full-content mode as the default and override it for individual feeds.

Stay on the keyboard

Move through articles, feeds, and groups; open originals or comments; and mark a view read with compact shortcuts.

See where stories come from

Source favicons, timestamps, direct headline links, and clear read state keep every article grounded in its original site.

Bring your subscriptions

Import and export OPML, then tune display and sort behavior without rebuilding your feed list.

Keep it local

A single Go service stores accounts, subscriptions, settings, and article state in a local SQLite database.

Run it yourself

feedss is distributed as cross-platform binaries and a multi-architecture container image. Point it at a persistent data directory, expose one port, and the reader is ready.

The interface works across desktop and narrow screens, automatically refreshes feeds on your schedule, and can notify administrators when a new feedss release is available.
